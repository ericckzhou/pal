# Code of Conduct

pal follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page summarises it; the full text at that link is what applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it gracefully.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, or sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub's [private reporting form](https://github.com/ericckzhou/pal/security/advisories/new), and put "Code of Conduct" in the title. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban.
